AivekAI Partner Program Terms & Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 · Operated by: Mozarex · Jurisdiction: Australia & International

These AivekAI Partner Program Terms & Policy ("Partner Terms") govern participation in the AivekAI Partner Program operated by Mozarex ("AivekAI", "we", "us", or "our").

By applying for, joining, or participating in the AivekAI Partner Program, you ("Partner", "you", or "your") agree to these Partner Terms.

These Partner Terms apply in addition to any other applicable AivekAI terms and policies.

1. The AivekAI Partner Program

The AivekAI Partner Program allows approved creators, influencers, coaches, nutrition professionals, publishers and other eligible partners to promote AivekAI and potentially earn commissions from qualifying customer subscriptions attributed to them.

Participation is subject to approval by AivekAI.

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance into the Partner Program.

AivekAI may approve, reject, suspend or terminate a Partner account in accordance with these Partner Terms.

2. Partner Eligibility

To participate, you must:

  • be at least 18 years old;
  • provide accurate and current information;
  • provide a valid email address and payout information;
  • have the legal capacity to enter into these Partner Terms;
  • comply with applicable advertising, consumer protection, tax and marketing laws;
  • comply with the rules of platforms on which you promote AivekAI; and
  • not use misleading, fraudulent or deceptive methods to generate referrals.

We may request additional information to verify your identity, business, promotional channels or eligibility before approving an application or processing a payout.

3. Partner Account

Approved Partners receive access to the AivekAI Partner Portal.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials.

You must not share your Partner Portal account with unauthorized persons.

You must notify us promptly if you believe your account or referral code has been compromised.

You are responsible for ensuring that your account information, including payout information, remains accurate.

4. Referral Code and Partner Link

Approved Partners may receive a unique referral code and/or referral link.

Example: Referral code: JAMES · Campaign Link: https://mozarex.com/aivekai?ref=JAMES

AivekAI may use third-party attribution technology, including Kochava SmartLinks, to identify customers who arrive through Partner links.

Partners may distribute their referral links or codes through approved channels such as social media, websites, videos, blogs and other promotional content, subject to these Partner Terms.

Referral codes and links remain associated with the AivekAI Partner Program and may be disabled or changed where reasonably necessary.

5. Customer Attribution

A customer may be attributed to a Partner when a valid Partner referral is successfully recorded by AivekAI.

Attribution may occur through:

  • a Partner referral link;
  • a referral code;
  • supported deep-link or deferred deep-link attribution; or
  • another attribution method approved by AivekAI.

AivekAI's systems and records will be used to determine referral attribution.

First Valid Referral

Where more than one Partner attempts to refer the same customer, the first valid referral successfully attributed to that customer will generally receive the attribution.

Once a customer has been validly attributed to a Partner, the referral is normally locked and cannot simply be overwritten by another Partner code.

We may correct attribution where there is clear evidence of technical error, fraud, manipulation or administrative error.

6. Customer Discounts

Some Partner referrals may make customers eligible for a promotional discount on an AivekAI subscription.

Discounts:

  • may vary by campaign, Partner, country or store;
  • may only apply to selected subscription products;
  • may be limited to the first subscription period;
  • may not apply to renewals; and
  • are subject to eligibility and availability through the applicable app store.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, Partner promotional discounts currently apply to eligible annual subscription offers, while monthly subscriptions remain at their normal applicable price.

The actual price displayed and charged by Apple App Store or Google Play is authoritative.

A Partner must not promise a price or discount that AivekAI or the applicable app store does not actually offer.

7. Partner Commission

Approved Partners may receive a commission on eligible revenue generated from qualifying customers attributed to them.

The applicable commission rate will be shown in the Partner Portal or otherwise communicated to the Partner.

Example: An approved Partner may have a 30% commission rate. The actual rate applicable to each Partner may differ.

AivekAI records the applicable commission rate when qualifying commission is generated so that subsequent changes to a Partner's commission rate do not improperly change historical commission calculations.

8. Recurring Commission

Where a Partner's offer includes recurring commission, the Partner may continue earning commission from eligible subscription revenue generated by their attributed customers while those subscriptions remain eligible under the Partner Program.

Recurring commission is not a guarantee of lifetime payment.

Commission eligibility remains subject to:

  • continued qualifying customer payments;
  • successful store transaction verification;
  • refunds and reversals;
  • these Partner Terms;
  • the Partner's account remaining eligible;
  • fraud and abuse controls; and
  • any specific campaign or commission terms communicated to the Partner.

9. What Counts as Eligible Revenue

Commission is calculated only from revenue that AivekAI recognizes as eligible under the Partner Program.

A displayed subscription, referral, click, install or estimated transaction does not by itself create an unconditional right to payment.

AivekAI verifies transactions using applicable platform/store information before commission becomes payable.

Commission may therefore progress through different states, including pending, available, allocated and paid.

10. Pending Commission

New commission may initially appear as Pending.

Pending commission is not yet available for withdrawal.

A pending amount may be awaiting:

  • transaction verification;
  • payment confirmation;
  • applicable holding periods;
  • refund/reversal periods;
  • fraud review; or
  • other financial reconciliation.

A pending commission does not constitute a final payable amount.

11. Available Commission

Commission becomes Available once the applicable verification and eligibility requirements have been satisfied.

Only available commission may be considered for payout, subject to the minimum payout threshold and other requirements.

12. Payout Threshold

A minimum payout threshold may apply.

The applicable threshold is displayed in the Partner Portal and may depend on the payout currency (e.g. A$100.00 / US$100.00).

If the Partner's available balance has not reached the applicable minimum threshold, the balance will normally remain in the Partner account until the threshold is reached.

13. PayPal Payouts

AivekAI currently uses PayPal as a Partner payout provider.

Partners are responsible for providing a valid PayPal payout destination.

The Partner must ensure that:

  • the PayPal account details are correct;
  • the account is capable of receiving the relevant payment;
  • the Partner is legally permitted to receive the payout; and
  • any PayPal verification requirements have been completed.

AivekAI is not responsible for delays resulting from incorrect Partner-provided payout information or restrictions imposed by PayPal.

14. Payout Fees

Unless otherwise stated in the Partner Portal or agreed with the Partner, AivekAI currently bears the PayPal provider payout fee separately from the Partner's approved commission amount.

Example: If a Partner has A$107.54 of approved commission and PayPal charges AivekAI a separate provider fee, the provider fee does not automatically reduce the Partner's A$107.54 commission.

AivekAI may change its payout fee policy prospectively by updating these Partner Terms or providing appropriate notice.

15. Refunds, Chargebacks and Reversals

Commission is earned only on valid, eligible revenue.

If a customer transaction is subsequently refunded, partially refunded, revoked, reversed, charged back, found to be fraudulent, or otherwise invalidated, the related Partner commission may be adjusted or reversed.

For a partial refund, the related commission may be adjusted proportionally.

Where commission has already been paid, the adjustment may be deducted from future Partner commissions or handled through another reasonable reconciliation process.

We will not intentionally charge commission on revenue that AivekAI did not validly retain.

16. Customer Cancellation

A customer cancelling their subscription does not automatically invalidate commission that was legitimately earned from eligible payments already completed.

However, no future recurring commission will be generated where there is no further qualifying subscription revenue.

17. Failed or Returned Partner Payouts

A payout may fail, be held, returned, blocked, unclaimed, reversed or otherwise delayed by PayPal.

Where this happens, AivekAI may hold the relevant commission while the payout is investigated or reconciled.

A returned or reversed payout will not automatically be sent again without appropriate verification.

Partners may be asked to correct their payout details before another payout attempt.

18. Taxes

Partners are responsible for determining and meeting their own tax obligations arising from commissions received through the Partner Program.

AivekAI does not provide tax advice.

Where legally required, AivekAI may request tax, identity, business or residency information and may withhold or report amounts as required by applicable law.

Partners should obtain independent professional advice regarding their circumstances.

19. Advertising Disclosure

Partners must clearly disclose their commercial relationship with AivekAI whenever required by applicable law or platform rules.

For example, content may need to clearly indicate that it contains an affiliate link, referral relationship, paid partnership or other commercial arrangement.

The disclosure must be sufficiently clear for an ordinary consumer to understand that the Partner may receive a benefit if they subscribe through the Partner's referral.

Partners must comply with applicable advertising rules in the countries where they promote AivekAI.

20. Marketing Standards

Partners must promote AivekAI accurately and responsibly.

Partners must not:

  • make false or misleading claims;
  • guarantee weight loss or specific health outcomes;
  • make unsupported medical or nutritional claims;
  • represent themselves as AivekAI employees;
  • misrepresent AivekAI features or capabilities;
  • advertise discounts that are not available;
  • use fake testimonials or reviews;
  • create fake accounts or transactions;
  • manipulate referrals or attribution;
  • use spam;
  • impersonate another person or business;
  • engage in deceptive advertising;
  • misrepresent commission arrangements;
  • encourage customers to misuse the service; or
  • violate applicable platform rules or laws.

21. Health and Weight-Loss Claims

Because AivekAI relates to food, nutrition and calorie tracking, Partners must take particular care with health-related marketing.

Partners must not claim that AivekAI:

  • diagnoses medical conditions;
  • treats or cures diseases;
  • guarantees weight loss;
  • replaces medical advice;
  • guarantees the accuracy of every calorie or nutrition estimate; or
  • produces guaranteed health outcomes.

AivekAI should be represented as a tool designed to assist users with tracking and understanding food, calorie and nutrition information.

22. Brand Usage

Partners may use approved AivekAI logos, screenshots, product descriptions and marketing materials solely for legitimate promotion of AivekAI under the Partner Program.

Partners must not:

  • modify the AivekAI brand in a misleading way;
  • imply ownership of the AivekAI brand;
  • register confusingly similar domains, social accounts or trademarks;
  • create websites or accounts that impersonate AivekAI; or
  • imply official endorsement beyond the Partner relationship.

All AivekAI intellectual property remains the property of its applicable owner.

23. Paid Advertising

Partners must not use paid advertising in a way that impersonates AivekAI or creates confusion about whether an advertisement is operated directly by AivekAI.

Unless expressly approved by AivekAI, Partners must not bid on AivekAI-branded search terms or misleading variations intended to intercept users already searching specifically for AivekAI.

AivekAI may impose additional paid-advertising restrictions for individual campaigns.

24. Self-Referrals and Artificial Transactions

Partners must not generate commission through artificial or manipulated transactions.

Prohibited conduct includes:

  • creating fake customers;
  • generating fake subscriptions;
  • repeatedly subscribing through your own referral solely to earn commission;
  • coordinating artificial purchases;
  • manipulating devices/accounts to generate referrals;
  • using stolen payment methods;
  • intentionally exploiting attribution systems; or
  • otherwise creating transactions without genuine customer intent.

AivekAI may withhold or reverse commission reasonably associated with fraudulent or abusive activity while it investigates.

25. Spam and Unsolicited Marketing

Partners must not promote AivekAI using unlawful spam, unsolicited bulk communications or other prohibited marketing practices.

Partners are responsible for complying with applicable email, SMS, privacy and electronic marketing laws.

AivekAI may review Partner activity for fraud, abuse, unusual referral patterns or violations of these Partner Terms.
