1. The AivekAI Partner Program

The AivekAI Partner Program allows approved creators, influencers, coaches, nutrition professionals, publishers and other eligible partners to promote AivekAI and potentially earn commissions from qualifying customer subscriptions attributed to them.

Participation is subject to approval by AivekAI.

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance into the Partner Program.

AivekAI may approve, reject, suspend or terminate a Partner account in accordance with these Partner Terms.

2. Partner Eligibility and Verification

To participate, you must:

  • be at least 18 years old;
  • provide accurate, truthful and current business and contact information;
  • provide a valid email address and verified payout information;
  • have the legal capacity to enter into these Partner Terms;
  • comply with applicable advertising, consumer protection, tax and marketing laws in every jurisdiction where you promote AivekAI;
  • comply with the rules of platforms on which you promote AivekAI; and
  • not use misleading, fraudulent or deceptive methods to generate referrals.

We may request additional information to verify your identity, business entity, promotional channels or eligibility before approving an application or processing a payout.

3. Partner Account & Portal Access

Approved Partners receive access to the AivekAI Partner Portal.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials.

You must not share your Partner Portal account with unauthorized persons.

You must notify us promptly if you believe your account or referral code has been compromised.

You are responsible for ensuring that your account information, including payout information, remains accurate.

4. Referral Codes, Links and Attribution Technology

Approved Partners may receive a unique referral code and/or referral link.

Example: Referral code: JAMES · Campaign Link: https://mozarex.com/aivekai?ref=JAMES

AivekAI may use third-party attribution technology, including Kochava SmartLinks, deep links, and deferred deep links, to identify customers who arrive through Partner links.

Partners may distribute their referral links or codes through approved channels such as social media, websites, videos, blogs and other promotional content, subject to these Partner Terms.

Referral codes and links remain associated with the AivekAI Partner Program and may be modified, disabled, or retired where reasonably necessary.

5. Customer Attribution & First Valid Referral

A customer may be attributed to a Partner when a valid Partner referral is successfully recorded by AivekAI's attribution systems.

Attribution may occur through:

  • a Partner referral link;
  • a referral code entered by the customer;
  • supported deep-link or deferred deep-link attribution; or
  • another attribution method approved and supported by AivekAI.

AivekAI's tracking systems and records are authoritative for determining referral attribution.

First Valid Referral Principle

Where more than one Partner attempts to refer the same customer, the first valid referral successfully recorded and attributed to that customer will receive the attribution.

Once a customer has been validly attributed to a Partner, the attribution is locked and cannot be overwritten by subsequent referral attempts.

AivekAI reserves the right to correct attribution where there is clear evidence of technical error, fraud, system manipulation, or administrative discrepancy.

  • are subject to eligibility and technical availability through the applicable application store.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, Partner promotional discounts currently apply to eligible annual subscription offers, while monthly subscriptions remain at their normal applicable price.

The actual price displayed and charged by Apple App Store, Google Play, or the authorized payment processor is authoritative.

Partners must not advertise, promise, or represent a price or discount that AivekAI or the applicable store does not actually offer.

7. Partner Commission Structure and Agreed Commission Rate

7.1 Definition of Agreed Commission Rate

The "Agreed Commission Rate" means the commission percentage rate currently agreed between AivekAI and the Partner and recorded in the Partner Portal or otherwise expressly confirmed in writing by AivekAI.

7.2 Partner-Specific Rates and Public Advertising

Different Partners or promotional tiers may have different Agreed Commission Rates. The public Partner Program landing page or promotional materials may advertise a current standard or indicative commission rate, but any such advertised rate is illustrative and does not override an individual Partner's Agreed Commission Rate recorded in their Partner account.

7.3 Rate-at-Transaction Commercial Model

Commission is calculated on a rate-at-transaction basis. Unless otherwise expressly agreed in writing, the commission rate applicable to any qualifying customer transaction, initial purchase, or subscription renewal is the Partner's Agreed Commission Rate in effect at the exact time that specific transaction or renewal occurs.

The commission rate in effect at the time a customer was originally referred does not create a perpetual or lifetime entitlement to that rate for subsequent subscription renewals. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, each subsequent renewal generates commission at the Partner's Agreed Commission Rate in effect at the time of that renewal.

7.4 Prospective Rate Updates

AivekAI may update or adjust a Partner's Agreed Commission Rate prospectively from time to time in accordance with business requirements and applicable law.

Where a material rate change affects an active Partner, AivekAI will provide reasonable advance notice through the Partner Portal, by email, or via another appropriate written channel.

A prospective rate change will never retrospectively reduce commission that has already been validly earned on transactions completed prior to the effective date of the rate change.

8. Recurring Commission Conditions

Where a Partner's account includes recurring commission eligibility, the Partner may continue to earn commission on eligible subscription payments and renewals generated by their attributed active customers at the Partner's applicable Agreed Commission Rate, provided that the Partner remains an active participant in good standing in the Partner Program.

Recurring commission is not a guarantee of lifetime payment. Commission eligibility remains strictly subject to:

  • continued qualifying customer payments;
  • successful verification and settlement of the underlying transaction;
  • deduction of applicable refunds, chargebacks, and reversals;
  • these Partner Terms and the Partner remaining an eligible participant in good standing;
  • compliance with fraud, anti-abuse, and marketing standards; and
  • any specific campaign, commercial, or tier conditions agreed in writing.

9. Eligible Revenue and Commission Calculation Basis

9.1 Eligible Net Revenue Calculation

Commission is calculated solely from Eligible Net Revenue recognized and retained by AivekAI from qualifying customer subscriptions attributed to the Partner.

Eligible Net Revenue represents gross customer subscription payments actually received by AivekAI, AFTER deducting:

  • applicable third-party platform, app-store, distribution, and payment-processing fees actually charged, withheld, or deducted by billing providers (including Apple App Store, Google Play, Stripe, or other transaction processors);
  • Value Added Tax (VAT), Goods and Services Tax (GST), sales taxes, consumption taxes, or other mandatory statutory transaction taxes collected or remitted;
  • introductory customer promotional discounts, coupons, trial deductions, or subsidized promotional credits; and
  • subsequent customer refunds, partial refunds, returns, chargebacks, disputed transactions, payment reversals, and fraudulent or invalidated transactions.

9.2 Commission Calculation Formula

The commission payable for an eligible transaction is calculated as:

Eligible Commission = Eligible Net Revenue × Partner's Agreed Commission Rate (%)

9.3 Commission Progression States

A displayed referral, install, click, or estimated transaction does not create an unconditional right to payment. Commission progresses through defined ledger states (Pending, Available, Allocated, Paid, and Reversed/Adjusted) and becomes payable only after verification, holding periods, and reconciliation requirements are satisfied.

10. Pending Commission & Holding Period

New commission initially appears as Pending.

Pending commission is subject to a standard holding period (typically 30 days) to allow for:

  • app-store settlement and financial confirmation;
  • customer refund and cancellation windows;
  • chargeback and dispute processing;
  • fraud, attribution, and anti-abuse verification; and
  • routine financial ledger reconciliation.

Pending commission does not constitute a finalized debt or payable balance until verified and transitioned to Available status.

11. Available Commission

Commission becomes Available once the applicable holding period, store reconciliation, and verification requirements have been fully satisfied.

Only available commission is eligible for payout inclusion, subject to reaching the applicable minimum payout threshold.

12. Minimum Payout Threshold

A minimum payout threshold applies before a payout can be generated.

The applicable threshold is displayed in the Partner Portal and configured per payout currency (e.g. A$100.00 / US$100.00).

If the Partner's available balance in a given currency has not reached the threshold, the balance remains safely in the Partner's account and rolls over until the minimum threshold is met.

AivekAI currently processes Partner payouts via PayPal Payouts.

Partners are responsible for providing and maintaining an active, verified PayPal account capable of receiving funds in the relevant currency.

The Partner must ensure that:

  • their PayPal payout email is accurate and up to date;

  • they are legally permitted to receive commercial payments in their jurisdiction.

AivekAI is not responsible for payout delays, holds, or rejections resulting from inaccurate Partner details, unverified PayPal accounts, or restrictions imposed directly by PayPal.

14. Payout Provider Fees

Unless otherwise stated in the Partner Portal or agreed in writing, AivekAI currently absorbs standard PayPal provider payout transaction fees separately from the Partner's approved commission balance.

Example: If a Partner has A$100.00 of approved available commission and PayPal charges AivekAI a provider payout transaction fee, that fee does not reduce the Partner's A$100.00 commission payout.

AivekAI reserves the right to modify its payout fee policy prospectively by updating these Partner Terms or providing reasonable written notice.

15. Refunds, Chargebacks, Adjustments and Clawbacks

Commission is earned only on valid, retained revenue.

If a customer payment is subsequently refunded, partially refunded, revoked, reversed, charged back, or determined to be fraudulent, the associated commission will be adjusted or reversed.

For partial refunds, commission is reduced proportionally.

Where commission has already been transitioned to Available status or paid out, AivekAI may offset the reversed amount against the Partner's future commissions through standard ledger adjustments.

16. Customer Subscription Cancellations

A customer cancelling their subscription does not invalidate commissions legitimately earned on completed subscription periods prior to cancellation.

However, no future recurring commission will be generated once customer billing ceases.

17. Failed, Returned or Held Payouts

If a payout is returned, blocked, unclaimed, or reversed by PayPal, AivekAI will hold the relevant funds while the issue is investigated.

Returned payouts will not be reissued until the Partner provides verified, corrected payout details.
