Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

Mozarex ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data & Health Profile

We may collect personally identifiable information and wellness characteristics, such as your name, email address, age, gender, height, weight, and primary health goals when you register for an account, complete onboarding, or update your profile. This information is used to estimate your daily calorie budgets, target macronutrients, and customize your coaching recommendations.

Device Permissions

We may request access to certain features from your mobile device, including:

  • Camera & Photos: Used to capture or upload images of your meals for visual AI nutritional analysis. Images are processed securely and are never shared publicly unless you choose to post them to the community feed.
  • Notifications: Used to send you daily reminders to log your meals and hydration, as well as community social updates.

You can change our access or permissions at any time in your device's settings.

App Activity Data

We collect data regarding your usage of the App, such as food logs, recipes, exercise logs, daily water consumption, weight entries, community posts, likes, and chat messages with your AI coach. This data is stored to track your progress and provide the core functionality of the App.

2. Use of Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Calculate personal metabolic targets (daily calorie budget, water intake, protein/carb/fat targets).
  • Generate real-time nutritional coaching feedback, meal recommendations, and exercise insights.
  • Synchronize your logs, weight history, and chat messages across multiple devices.
  • Enable community social features, including public profile viewing and feed sharing.
  • Send you reminders and notifications regarding your daily routines.
  • Moderate community posts for safety and compliance with content guidelines.
  • Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve your experience with the App.

3. Third-Party Services

We may share information with third parties that perform services for us or on our behalf, including data analysis, hosting services, customer service, and AI processing. We only share personal data with third parties that provide equivalent or equal protection. Specifically, we use:

OpenAI (Third-Party AI Service)

AivekAI integrates with OpenAI's API to analyze your food descriptions, scan meal photos, estimate nutrition values, and generate personalized coaching feedback. We obtain your explicit consent before transmitting any data to OpenAI. If consent is granted, the following details may be securely sent to OpenAI (api.openai.com):

  • Your meal descriptions, text messages, and exercise logs.
  • Photos of food/meals that you choose to scan.
  • Basic wellness characteristics (age, weight, height, gender, and primary goals).

OpenAI processes this data strictly for generating your real-time responses and does not use API inputs to train OpenAI models. You can toggle or revoke your consent at any time in the app settings.

Supabase

We use Supabase for secure cloud backend services, including:

  • Authentication: To manage user accounts securely.
  • Database: To store your profile data, daily calorie/water goals, weight history, chat logs, and community posts.

Kochava

We use Kochava for app analytics and measurement to understand user signups, onboarding completions, and feature engagement. Kochava collects anonymized device identifiers and app events in compliance with privacy guidelines.
